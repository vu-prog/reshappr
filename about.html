About

Built by a barber who did it from a house.

Reshappr makes online courses and digital products for barbers, and runs a done-with-you mentorship for the ones who want somebody working the system with them.

Why this exists

Barbers copy the video instead of the reason the video worked, and that is the entire gap.

A reel does four million views and half the barbers in the country film the same transition that week. The transition was never the part doing the work. It was the message sitting underneath it, which is invisible from the outside, so the copy is dead before it gets posted. Nobody teaches that layer because most of the people selling barber courses cannot see it either.

Vu Tran worked it out the slow way, cutting out of a house and taking his book from around a thousand a month to eighteen thousand over six months. At the peak that was about thirty-two cuts a week in thirty-five hours, none of it from a shop, a suite, or a dollar of ad spend.

He stopped taking clients in October 2025 to teach it full time. Reshappr came out of that: the courses, the digital products, and a mentorship where someone works the system with you instead of selling you a login and going quiet.

Vu Tran Founder, Reshappr

What the work runs on

Five things the coaching keeps coming back to, on the calls and in the content.

Excellence. The standard is work you would put your name on with nobody watching. That covers the haircut and the video you post about it.
Perseverance. Most barbers stop posting around week three, which is usually right before it starts moving. The daily calls exist because that is where people fall off.
Gratefulness. The clients sitting in your chair right now are the proof you show the ones you want next. Treat them like it.
Value. The information goes out free. What you pay for is somebody working it with you five days a week, not a secret nobody else has.
Honesty. No borrowed screenshots, no invented client numbers, and no guarantee about your income that nobody could actually keep.
