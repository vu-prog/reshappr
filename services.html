The Barber Mentorship

120 days, done with you.

Reshappr sells online courses and digital products for barbers. The mentorship is the version where you get the whole library plus somebody working through it with you on the phone five days a week.

What comes with it

The members portal. Every course module, framework, and template, yours to work through at your own pace between calls.
A group coaching call every weekday. Monday through Friday at 7:00 PM EST. Bring what you are stuck on that day rather than sitting on it for a week.
A weekly one on one. Your account, your city, your numbers, on a call that is only about you.
Access between the calls. Message a question when it comes up and get an answer, instead of guessing until the next session.
An onboarding strategy session. The first call maps where you actually are and sets your start date.

What is in the portal

Mindset and the first 30 days

What the opening month actually looks like, the non-negotiables you hold to daily, and why the barbers who make it through week three are usually the ones who set the bar before they started.

Profile and booking funnel

Bio formula, Facebook page, the link setup, and the fixes people get wrong across all four platforms. The goal is a stranger going from your video to a booked appointment without messaging you.

Posting strategy

What to post, when, and on which platform, plus the caption template. Simple on purpose, because the barbers who overthink this are the ones who post twice and stop.

The 4-part content framework

The structure underneath videos that convert, and the re-hooks that hold someone past the first three seconds. This is the why that copying a trend never gives you.

The before and after showcase

Where week one starts, because your work can sell for you before you are comfortable talking on camera. Filming, angles, and putting the two halves together.

The consultation masterclass

The question sequence you run before you pick up a clipper. It is the difference between hoping someone accepts your price and them asking to book the next one.

Editing walkthroughs, sound and song libraries, and the small details from Vu's last hundred videos sit in there too, and modules keep getting added.

How the 120 days run

01

Onboarding call

We go through your account, your city, and what you charge now. That call sets your start date.

02

Profile and funnel

Bio, Facebook page, booking link. Someone who lands on your page books in two taps.

03

Post every day

Showcases first, frameworks once the daily habit holds. The calls keep you honest about the reps.

04

Consultation and price

The sequence you run in the chair, so raising your rate stops feeling like an apology.

Who this is not for

Worth reading before you book, because a call neither of us needed wastes your evening.

You want a shortcut where the views do the work and you do not have to change how you operate.
You are not going to post consistently. The daily reps are the program, and nothing here works around them.
You are looking for haircut technique coaching. This is the business around the haircut.
You are not willing to look stupid on camera for a while before it starts working.
