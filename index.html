FAQ

Straight answers to fair questions.

Do I need a shop or a suite first?
No. Vu built his book to $18K months cutting out of a house, and the mentorship is written for barbers doing the same thing. Where you cut matters far less than whether somebody who finds you online can actually book you.
Do I have to go viral for this to work?
No, and chasing that is usually what keeps barbers stuck. There are barbers sitting at fifty thousand views with a half empty week, which tells you reach was never the thing in the way. The message underneath the video is what turns a viewer into somebody sitting in your chair.
I bought a barber course before and it barely scratched the surface. Why is this different?
Most of them hand you the visible layer of a video and stop there. You get the reasoning underneath, which is the part that transfers to your city and your work, plus a coach on the phone every weekday while you apply it. Nobody grades your homework in a course.
Do you teach cutting technique?
No. This is the business built around the haircut: your profile, your content, your consultation, and what you charge. Bring your own hands.
How much time does this take?
Posting daily and showing up to the calls. If you cannot commit to posting every day for the length of the program, wait until you can, because the daily reps are the whole thing.
How long is the program?
120 days, and it starts on the date we set during your onboarding call rather than the day you sign up.
Do you guarantee I will make a certain number?
No, and anyone handing you a number is guessing at your city, your skill, and how much you will actually do. What is on the table is the system Vu ran himself and a coach working it with you five days a week.
